## Consider joining Tarry!
Tampereen Rytmipelaajat ry is a Finnish non-profit association here to make Tampere a more rhythmical place. We work with [rytmiepelit](https://tarry.fi/rytmiepelit) to bring rhythm games to cons and events, organize dance game tournaments and much more! If you're interested in our quest, you can join our official [Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/DvfRy8ebh9) to talk with us or come play at our [arcade](https://tarry.fi/arcade)!!! 👀 (opens in January 2026)


As per Finnish association law, to join Tarry, you must accept our rules and pay the yearly membership fee.

[Registered rules of the association](http://tarry.fi/rules.pdf)

Membership fee 2025: 10€<br>
Pay with ease at our [online store](https://holvi.com/shop/tarry-store)
