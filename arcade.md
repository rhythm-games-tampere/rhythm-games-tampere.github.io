Tarry will have an arcade in [Nekalab](https://www.tampere.fi/organisaatio/nekalan-kulttuurikeskus-nekalab) in January 2026. The space will feature rhythm game classics such as ITGmania.

## How do I get in?
Tarry's arcade's doors are open for everyone on a certain undecided day perhaps weekly or biweekly. You can also ping a tenant on Discord to see if they'll agree to share a session with you. They're very friendly so they probably will. 

## Arriving at the Arcade
Nekalab is located at Lounaantie 2, Tampere. The building is an old school and the nearest bus stops are Lampipuisto and Kuokkamaantie, reachable from the city center with bus lines 10 and 50 respectively.

The arcade is located on the bottom floor, so walk through the main entrance and head right. You won't miss it.
